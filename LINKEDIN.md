Introducing **PressFlow V2 — from quotation to payment.**

Built for the practical flow of a printing business: quotations, confirmed orders,
partial deliveries, delivery-linked invoices and dated payment records.

The focus was more than a cleaner screen:
- keep delivery, invoicing and payment status separate;
- preserve issued documents and traceable corrections;
- handle stale updates and uncertain save acknowledgements;
- make the workflow usable on mobile, with light/dark themes and A4 output.

Stack: JavaScript, Netlify Functions and Neon PostgreSQL.

**Designed & developed by MBS.**

This video uses fictional demo records. V2 product showcase; production acceptance
is still a separate gate. Cloud mode requires internet.
